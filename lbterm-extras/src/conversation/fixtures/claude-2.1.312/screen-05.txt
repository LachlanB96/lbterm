[38:2::255:255:255m[49m●[39m 1. A scrollbar is a control that shows which part of a longer document is visible.
  2. The track is the full length of the scrollable area.
  3. The thumb is the draggable piece inside the track.
  4. Its size is the visible area divided by the total content height.
  5. A large thumb means little content is hidden; a small one means a lot is.
  6. Its position is the scroll offset divided by the scrollable range.
  7. Dragging the thumb changes the scroll offset.
  8. Clicking the track jumps one screen-height up or down.
  9. Arrow buttons at each end scroll by a small fixed step.
  10. The mouse wheel and trackpad send scroll events that change the same offset.
  11. The app redraws only the lines that fall inside the new window.
  12. The thumb is recalculated whenever the content or window size changes.
  13. When content fits the window, the scrollbar is hidden or disabled.
  14. Terminals keep a scrollback buffer of past lines so the bar has something to scroll through.
  15. Scrolling changes which slice of that buffer is drawn, not the buffer itself.
