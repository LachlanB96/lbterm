# Tabs with the mouse

Everything done to a tab can be done with the mouse, in the tab panel or the tab bar when it shows tabs.

- **Move a tab:** press on it and drag along the panel (or the bar). It moves to the slot under the pointer as it goes, as the tab bar's own moves do. A press that wobbles less than 6 px is still a click. On the panel the row is lifted (built 2026-10-21): it follows the pointer with a soft shadow, over the others, which slide out of its way, and slides into its slot when it's let go (`lbterm/TAB-PANEL.md`).
- **Give it a window of its own:** drag it out of the window and let go at least 24 px away from it. The new window opens with its top-left where you let go, and takes the keyboard. Let go nearer than that and the tab stays where the drag left it. Once the pointer is past the window the tab stops moving along the panel, so it leaves from its own slot. The only tab in a window has nowhere to go and stays. Past that distance a small card with the tab's name follows the pointer, where the window would open (`wezterm-gui/src/tab_ghost.rs`, built 2026-10-21): a layered popup of its own that never activates or takes the keyboard, and that the mouse goes through, so the drag keeps the pointer. It goes when the pointer comes back near the window or the tab is let go.
- **Move it into another LBTerm window:** let go over that window. The tab becomes its active tab and the window comes forward. While the pointer is over it, that window's rail rings an empty slot after its last tab in the accent, where the tab will land (`lbterm_panel_drop_hint`).
- **Make it wait on another tab:** press the right button on its row in the panel and drag onto another tab's row (lbterm/LINKS.md, with `lbterm_links`): a line joins their pads and the dragged tab's pad turns purple. A right press that doesn't move is still the tab menu, opening as it's let go. Resting on a line opens a card saying who waits on whom, where it stands and for what, without opening the panel. With Shift held as it's let go, the drag connects the two tabs instead (LINKS.md, "Connections"), cyan while Shift is held.
- **The tab menu:** right-click a tab (in the panel or the bar, where upstream opened the tab navigator). A card in the panel's style opens at the pointer: dark, ringed in the panel's red light, in the panel's font. In the panel it opens as the button comes up, since a right press there may become a link's drag; in the tab bar, on the press.
  - **A tab's links**, under a rule of their own when it has any (lbterm/LINKS.md): Stop waiting on each tab it waits on, "<what it waits for> is now complete by <that tab>" to tell it to carry on now, Wait on it again where a link went wrong, and a suggested link to make or dismiss. Then its connections (LINKS.md, "Connections"): Leave the connection with the others for each one made, and Connect with or Decline each one suggested. The menu widens to fit the longest.
  - **Rename tab** (R): the name is typed in place on the row. It starts as the name the tab shows, the label under its pad (render/tab_panel.rs, `tab_label`: the name it was given, else its Claude session's label, else its title as its row reads it, "pwsh" for a shell whose pane says "pwsh.exe"), read as Rename is picked (lbterm/NEW-TAB.md, section 7, row 1, built 2026-11-05). Before that it started from the tab's own title, else its active pane's, so a Claude tab with no name given started from Claude Code's title and a test window's from "pwsh.exe". All of it is selected (highlighted in the panel's red), as Windows' rename boxes do: typing replaces it, Backspace or Delete clears it, and an arrow, Home or End keeps it to edit from its end. Enter keeps the new name, Esc goes back to the menu. Enter on an untouched name changes nothing, and an empty name gives the tab back the title its program sets.
  - **Colour:** a row of swatches (none, then red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, pink). The colour shows as a ring round the tab's status pad, in the closed rail and the open panel, beside its Claude status rather than over it.
  - **Copy link** (L), on a Claude tab: `[<label>](lbterm://session/<full id>) in <Project>` on the clipboard (`menu::copy_link`; the label `tab_label` gives as it's picked, the session the tab's as the menu opened, the project as New session names it, left off when there's none or the label ends with it), and the tab's pad glints (`light.glint`, copy_in and copy_out, as a rename's does). With the state file on, the text goes to the state file's `copied` instead and the clipboard is never touched (`copy_to_clipboard` asks `lbterm_copy_to_state` first, for every copy a window makes; lbterm/STATE.md): a test window shares Lachlan's clipboard (lbterm/PITFALLS.md, 151).
  - **New session in <Project>** (N), when the tab's folder is in one of `lbterm_projects`: Claude in the tab's folder, in front. The menu fades out (pane_exit) and in the same turn of the GUI it's a pick, as the picker's in front: the Starting row at the end of the tabs from the next frame, and `lbterm-project-action (window, pane, p)` with `p.action = 'new'`, `p.source = 'tab menu'`, `p.name` the project, `p.dir` the tab's folder, `p.project_dir` the project's own and `pane` the tab's active pane (render/new_tab.rs, `lbterm_new_tab_session_in`). Lachlan's lbterm.lua opens it through Conductor with `--by "tab menu"` (landing 1's handler); with no handler taking it, LBTerm opens PowerShell in that folder. The picker's recent picks aren't touched.
  - **Shell in its folder** (S; Duplicate tab until landing 2 of lbterm/NEW-TAB.md, and it does the same: it never duplicated a Claude session): a new PowerShell tab in the same folder and domain, its Starting row at the end of the tabs the next frame until it comes (lbterm/NEW-TAB.md). Its folder is the active pane's (OSC 7, else its youngest process's), as Duplicate's was, where New session's prefers the session's statusline's.
  - **Wait on…** (W), on a Claude tab with `lbterm_links` set while another Claude tab is on the rail: the card's rows dissolve on `swap` into a page of its own (the card's two surfaces swap round, the old page's last frame fading out over the new, `menu.tab.swap`; at once with motion off), its first row ‹ Back, then every other Claude tab but the Conductor's by its label, each after a small pad in its pad's colour as the rail draws it now (blue for one that's handed the turn back, orange for one that asks, purple for one that waits on another). Picking one makes this tab wait on it, or stop waiting there if it did: `lbterm-tab-link {action='toggle', waiter, on}`, the two sessions, as a right drag let go on that row does (tab_links.rs, `lbterm_link_wait_on`, with the objective its wait names when the wait is on that tab), and the menu fades out. ‹ Back, Left or Esc turns the card back to the menu, the same dissolve. The page's rows have no keys; the arrows and Enter work on them (built 2026-11-05, lbterm/NEW-TAB.md, section 7, row 7).
  - **Move to new window:** as dragging it out, with the window where Windows puts new ones.
  - **Close finished tabs (N)** (F), while one is finished: every Claude tab but the Conductor's whose row says Done or Your turn, with no unread white dot, no agents or tasks running, no tab link it waits through (a tab waiting on another isn't finished, however it handed the turn back: links.ts means to tell it to carry on, PITFALLS 153) and its objectives' green dot. A second line under its words, white at 50%, names them by label, cut to the card's width. Picked, it closes at once, without asking, every tab it named that's still finished (one that started working, asked something or got a new result since the menu opened stays, and one that finished since isn't taken unseen), each marked closed by hand and kept to reopen as Close tab's is. They all go in one turn, so each row fades where it was while the rows below slide up on one spring, and when the open tab was one, the next comes forward with its panes dissolving in, and when that row ends in the closed one's slot, the usual case, the marker rides up with it on its spring, so the ring stays on the open row the whole way (Lachlan's 3b of 2026-11-07; it waited in the slot while the row slid up under it before, as main did for Ctrl+W), or glides on `focus_move` when the open row doesn't move (FLOWS.md, "The tab menu's facts, and a pick from it", step 10): the rail's own motion for a tab closed (built 2026-11-05, lbterm/NEW-TAB.md, section 7, row 10).
  - **Close other tabs:** closes them at once, whatever runs in them, as Close tab does (Lachlan's yes, 2026-10-25). Every close that still asks (Ctrl+Shift+W, CloseCurrentTab with `confirm`, the tab bar's close button) folds the panel first, so the question shows where it'll stay: left open, the panel pressed the panes right, then folded once the pointer was off it on the way to the question, and the pane slid back left with the Yes under the pointer (Lachlan, 2026-10-25).
  - **Close tab:** closes it at once, whatever runs in it, without bringing it forward: picking it from the menu is the answer to the question it used to ask (Lachlan, 2026-10-25).
  - Every close of a tab by hand (Close tab, Close other tabs, Close finished tabs, a middle click on its row, Ctrl+W, the Yes of Ctrl+Shift+W on a tab's only pane: the closes that write close marks, termwindow/last_tab.rs) keeps it to reopen, the last ten in memory, with its label, its Claude sessions and its folder (lbterm/NEW-TAB.md, "Reopen"). A tab whose program ends by itself, a window closing and quitting keep nothing.
  - The arrow keys move through the rows (left and right along the swatches), Enter picks the row lit, or with none lit lights the first, Esc or a click outside closes it. The highlight covers the Colour row too, gliding there when Down or Up lands on a swatch or the pointer rests on one, the swatch's ring saying which (Lachlan's 12 of 2026-11-08; `menu::same_row`); the pointer on the word "Colour", off the swatches, lights nothing, since the row does nothing there. Each row's letter shows dim at its right (white 35%) and picks it, a digit picks a swatch (0 none, 1 to 8 the colours), and Delete is Close tab; a pick flashes its row white (`menu.list.flash`, Lachlan's 9b of 2026-11-07). A key no row takes flares the card's edge, its hairline going white and the light round the card going white under its red, rising on `arrive_in` and fading on `panel_glow`, about 0.3 s to a tenth of its peak at real speed (Lachlan's 9a of 2026-11-08: the one-pixel pulse it replaced was too faint to read; DESIGN.md, "Menus"), and nothing a menu takes reaches the pane (`menu::answer`, below). While it's open it has every key, those bound in the config too (Lachlan's 7b of 2026-11-07): a bound key does nothing but flare the edge, and acts again once the menu has closed, a menu fading out included (FLOWS.md, "A key while a menu is open"; PITFALLS 150). The one exception is the + menu's: a key it shows beside a row as that row's shortcut picks the row (his 5a of 2026-11-08; NEW-TAB.md). While it's open it takes every key and mouse event, but the pointer's moves still reach the tab panel's hover, the menu's card counting as the panel, and nothing of the rail behind it plays while any menu's card shows, open or fading out (his 7b of 2026-11-08): the panel's edge, light and wave, and its rows' own motion and the pads' too, so Close tab's row exit, the slide of the rows below and the marker's ride with them, Copy link's glint and a status word's dissolve wait under the card and play in the first frame after it has gone, about 120 ms after the key at real speed. Off both, the rail closes once the card has gone; a menu opened from the rail keeps it open until then (his 10a of 2026-11-08) (the Conductor's menu too: `tab_panel_hover_under_menu`; `Hover::hold` and `lbterm_motion::rail::Hold`, FLOWS.md, "A menu, from what opens it to the end of its fade", step 10).

- **Close a tab:** a middle click on its row in the tab panel closes it at once, without asking, as the menu's Close tab does: its Claude sessions' close marks written and the tab kept to reopen, so Ctrl+Shift+T brings it back (Lachlan's 10a, built 2026-11-05; render/tab_panel.rs, `tab_panel_mouse`'s `MousePress::Middle` arm). Only the row under the pointer, and only the tab that row was drawn for (`tab_panel_drawn_tab`: between a close and the next paint the tab below has slid into the row, PITFALLS 156): on the Conductor's pad or the + pad a middle click does nothing. On the open tab, the marker goes to the next as for Ctrl+W and Close finished tabs: it rides up with that tab's row when the row slides into the closed one's slot, and glides when the open row doesn't move (the last row's, a row up). On screen the ring and bar step onto that tab's pad a slot below at the close and ride up with it (FLOWS.md, "The tab menu's facts, and a pick from it", step 10's What could go: seen 2026-11-07). The last tab gives way to a new one while the Conductor's pad stays, as for every close (The last tab, below).
- **Open a tab:** the + pad at the rail's foot (lbterm/NEW-TAB.md). A left click opens the picker beside the rail: type a project the way @names are found and Enter opens Claude there; a click on a row is Enter, Shift+click opens it behind, Ctrl+click a shell. A right click opens the + menu at the pointer, upward, as the button comes up: the launch menu's shells in the open tab's folder, Shell in a project… (the picker for a shell), the tabs closed lately to reopen, and New window. Its rows follow the pointer and the arrow keys, Enter or a row's letter picks, Esc or a click outside closes it.
- **Reopen a tab closed:** the + menu's Reopen rows (up to five, newest first), or `LbtermNewTab 'Reopen'` for the newest (Ctrl+Shift+T in Lachlan's config). It fires `lbterm-reopen-tab (window, pane, {session, sessions, dir, label, project})` and leaves the list; with no handler returning false, LBTerm opens the default program in its folder, in front, its Starting row showing until it comes.

## The tab menu's model

`lbterm-extras/src/menu.rs` decides the menu's rows from what the GUI knows of the tab as the menu opens (`menu::Facts`), and is tested on its own (`lbterm.ps1 test -Package lbterm-extras`, `menu::tests`, kept in `menu/tests.rs`). Landing 2 of lbterm/NEW-TAB.md (section 7, "The tab menu, in order") builds it in stages on feat/new-tab-menus: the model first (2026-11-05), then the drawing, then the GUI gathering the facts and acting on the new rows. `lbterm_open_tab_menu` gathers every fact as the menu opens (stage 5, 2026-11-05): the tab's Claude session (`claude`, Copy link), its project (`project`, New session), its link rows, whether `lbterm_links` is set (`links_on`) and the rail's tabs (`tab_panel_menu_rail`, below), from which `menu::Facts::from_rail` takes Wait on…'s `others` and Close finished tabs' `finished`.

- **Rows, in groups under rules** (`menu::rows(facts, page)`; `menu::items` without the rules): Rename tab, Colour, Copy link | New session in <Project>, Shell in its folder | Park tab (or Unpark tab), Hide from Conductor (or Show to Conductor), Wait on… | the tab's link rows | Move to new window | Close finished tabs (N), Close other tabs, Close tab. A group with no rows takes no rule, so a rule is never first, last or beside another (the test runs all 256 mixes of the facts). Hide from Conductor came with landing 3 (2026-11-09; NEW-TAB.md, section 8): `Item::Hide`, its words from `Facts::conductor` (`conductor::Offer`: Hide, or Show while the tab looks hidden). Park tab came with landing 4 (2026-11-09; NEW-TAB.md, "Landing 4", 8a): `Item::Park`, its words from `Facts::park` (`menu::park_words`: Unpark tab on a parked tab), picked through wezterm-gui's `park.rs` (`set_parked`).
- **Which are there:** Copy link on a Claude tab (`Facts::claude`); Hide from Conductor on a Claude tab whose `Facts::conductor` the GUI set (render/conductor_hide.rs's `lbterm_hide_offer`: the Conductor's button.json has `hidden`, it isn't the Conductor's own tab, and no pick for the tab waits for the Conductor's word, `hide::Picks::offer`, PITFALLS 244); New session when the tab's folder is in a project (`project`, the name the picker gives it, which the row shows); Wait on… on a Claude tab with `lbterm_links` set (`links_on`) and another Claude tab open (`others`); Close finished tabs while one qualifies (`finished`, its count in the row's words); Park tab on any tab, a shell's too, whose `Facts::park` the GUI set (render/tab_menu.rs's `lbterm_open_tab_menu`: not the Conductor's tab, and `park::can_park`, which a mux domain whose server is from before 8a fails). Rename, Colour, Shell, Move to new window, Close other tabs and Close tab always.
- **Keys** (`menu::key`, `menu::by_key`, `menu::answer`): R, L, N, S, P, H, W, M, F, O and X, one a row, in either case, each drawn dim at its row's right in `Layout::key_box` (white 35%, as the + menu's hints; not on the Rename row while its name is typed). 0 picks no colour, 1 to 8 the colours in `tabs::COLOURS`' order. A key goes only to a row that's there: `by_key` gives `None` for one no row takes (L on a shell tab, F with nothing finished), the moment the card's edge flares once (the hairline to white and the light round the card white under its red, up on `arrive_in` and down on `panel_glow`: `lbterm_motion::menu::bump`, played on the compositor's `MenuLamp`; render/menu_fade.rs, `lbterm_menu_bump`). The link rows and Wait on…'s page have no keys. `menu::answer(key, page, items, focus)` is the whole keyboard outside a typed name, as a `menu::Did` the GUI carries out (render/tab_menu.rs, `lbterm_tab_menu_key`): a letter or digit picks; Delete picks Close tab; Enter picks the lit row or, with none lit, lights the first (FEEDBACK.md's gap, closed 2026-11-05); Esc closes, or on Wait on…'s page goes back, as Left does there; the arrows step (`menu::step`), lighting the first or last row from nothing; a modifier alone does nothing; anything else (a letter no row has, a key with Ctrl or Alt, Tab, a function key) is `Did::Nowhere`, the edge's flare. render/menu_fade.rs's `menu_key` turns the GUI's key into a `menu::Key`: a character counts only with no Ctrl, Alt or Windows key. The state file gives each item its key (`menu.items[].key`, lbterm/STATE.md).
- **Close finished tabs** (`menu::finished`, over `menu::RailTab`, one for each tab on the rail): every Claude tab but the Conductor's whose row's status word is Done or Your turn (so not Question, Extras, Permission, Login, Blocked, Failed, Working or Stuck), with no unread dot, no agents (`Row::agents`) or tasks (`Row::waiting`, the arc) running, and its objectives' green dot (`objectives::Mark::Done`), in the rail's order. The open tab is one of them when it qualifies. The row's second line lists them by label (`menu::finished_words`), cut to `Layout::second`'s width by `menu::finished_line`, which takes the widths the GUI measures (`lbterm_new_tab_width`, PITFALLS 76) and finds the longest cut that fits by halving (`menu::cut_to`: a few measures, where `lbterm_new_tab_fit` tries one character fewer at a time); the GUI cuts it once as the menu opens (render/tab_menu.rs, `lbterm_menu_second`, kept in `Open::second`), draws it white at 50% and gives it to the state file as the row's `detail`. Picked, it closes `menu::to_close(listed, rail)`: the tabs it listed that still qualify on the rail read again at the pick, in the rail's order (`lbterm_close_finished`: `lbterm_mark_tab` and `lbterm_remove_tab` for each, in one turn of the GUI).
- **Wait on…'s page** (`Page::WaitOn`): ‹ Back, then the other Claude tabs by label (`menu::others`: every Claude tab on the rail but the menu's own and the Conductor's, in the rail's order), each with its pad's colour as the rail draws it now (`Other::colour`). Picking Wait on… turns the page and ‹ Back, Left or Esc turns it back (`menu::answer`'s `Did::Back`), each a dissolve on `swap` (`lbterm_menu_turn`, the compositor's `menu_turn`); the card keeps the corner it opened at, though a page of another height flips up from it again when it opened near the window's foot. Each tab's row draws its pad's dot in `Layout::dot_box` (`menu::DOT`, 10 px at 96 DPI, corners of 3) and its words after it (`words_box`, the dot's room measured into `widest`). Picking a tab reads its session again (`tab_session`) and fires `lbterm-tab-link {action='toggle', waiter, on}` through tab_links.rs's `lbterm_link_wait_on`, as a right-drag's drop does.
- **Copy link** (`menu::copy_link`): `[<label>](lbterm://session/<full id>) in <Project>`, the project left off when there's none or when the label already ends with its name as a word of its own, in any case ("InMail · Lantern" stays as it is; "Heatwave" still gets " in Wave").
- **Layout** (`menu::layout`): as wide as its widest row as the title font lays it out, a key's room (`menu::KEY`, 22 px at 96 DPI) included, inside the window (`menu::widest`, measured by the GUI with render/new_tab.rs's `lbterm_new_tab_width`, kept by text, font and shape generation, once as the menu opens and again as its page turns, never on a pointer move, and never as characters times `cell_width`: PITFALLS 76). It was 260 px unless a link row's characters times `cell_width` were wider, which made a link's card wider than its words needed. Close finished tabs' row is a line taller, its second line (`Layout::second`) right under its words. `Layout::words_box` and `key_box` say where a row's words and its key go.

Where the GUI finds each fact (read as the menu opens, nothing on a pointer move):

- The tab's Claude session: `tab_session(tab)` in render/tab_panel.rs (the active pane's `claude_session` user var, else any pane's). Copy link's id is the same. Gathered (`Open::session`).
- Its project: render/new_tab.rs's `tab_folder(tab)` (its session's statusline's folder, `cwd.txt` through `crate::summaries`, else its active pane's, as `newtab::folder_of` spells it), then `lbterm_project_of(dir)` (`projects::Folders::of` over the config's projects, kept by render/projects.rs's `lbterm_project_folders` until the list changes: the deepest project folder holding it). That's the picker's Here row's folder and name for the open tab, and the name a closed tab is kept under for Reopen. Gathered (`Open::dir`, `Facts::project`).
- Whether it can be hidden from the Conductor, or shown to it again: render/conductor_hide.rs's `lbterm_hide_offer(tab)`, from every pane's `claude_session` (`tab_sessions`), the Conductor's file as the `lbterm-state-files` thread last read it (`lbterm_conductor_said`, a lock and an `Arc` clone) and the window's picks waiting for the Conductor's word (`TabPanelState::hides`). Gathered (`Facts::conductor`). A pick fires `lbterm-conductor-hide` and shows on the row until the file says it or 5 s go by (NEW-TAB.md, section 8).
- Its label as the row shows it: render/tab_panel.rs's `tab_label(tab, idx)` (`lbterm_panel::claude::row` over `panel_tab`), read when Rename, Copy link or Hide from Conductor is picked (the hide's event's `label`, and the strip's words if the Conductor doesn't do it).
- The rail's tabs, for Wait on… and Close finished tabs: render/tab_panel.rs's `tab_panel_menu_rail()`, gathered (stage 5). In `tab_panel_rail()`'s order (the Conductor's tab, `lbterm_panel.conductor_tab`, isn't on it), one `menu::RailTab` for each tab the last paint drew a row for, from the panel's `drawn` map (each tab's `lbterm_panel::claude::Tab` as that paint made its row, its `seen` time and any tab link laid over): `lbterm_panel::claude::row(tab, now, idle_full)`, the row as the rail draws it, link and all, gives its label (the one under its pad, the name it was given winning), its pad's colour (`palette::pad(row.state)`), its status word, unread dot, agents and tasks (`Row::status`, `unread`, `agents`, `waiting`), and `linked` is whether the drawn tab has a link (`Tab::link`: one the links file holds, or one the GUI asked of it and draws already). A linked tab is never finished (PITFALLS 153); stage 5 took the status with the link taken off, citing PITFALLS 52, which is about placeholders reaching the program, and Close finished tabs closed a tab that waited on another. Its session is `tab_session`'s, its mark `crate::summaries::get(&sid)`'s `objectives.mark()`. A tab the rail hasn't drawn isn't listed, so with the panel off there's no Wait on… and no Close finished tabs. A row's `unread` needs the tab never shown since its result came: `seen` is the time the panel last drew it as the active tab.

## Through a mux server

Tabs in a mux server's domain (`lbterm-mux-server`, lbterm/MUX.md) belong to the server's windows, so tearing one off or moving it asks the server to move its active pane (`MovePaneToNewTab`, as `lbterm cli move-pane-to-new-tab` does): a tab with splits leaves them behind. That makes a new tab on the server, so the GUI gives it the old one's name and colour, and the window the server makes for a torn-off tab opens where it was let go, as in the GUI's own domain. A renamed tab's title goes to the server too, so another GUI attached to it sees the new name. Moving along the panel is the GUI's own order and works the same in either.

Two things only a mux server's tabs do:

- Upstream's client domain, after a move, adds the new tab but leaves the pane in its old local tab as well, so the panel would show it twice. LBTerm takes the pane out of the old tab (without killing it) and drops the tab once it's empty.
- A pane in a server can't say what it's running (`ClientPane` always answers "can't close without asking"), so Ctrl+Shift+W always asks there. Close tab and Close other tabs never ask.

## The last tab

The Conductor's pad tops the rail and stands for its tab, or with a seat for its view, so to Lachlan it's a tab, and closing every other one mustn't close LBTerm (his report, 2026-10-25: it closed "as it thinks there's no tabs"). So while the pad is on the rail, closing the last tab of the last window has a new tab take its place first (`lbterm_extras::conductor::keeps_window`, `wezterm-gui/src/termwindow/last_tab.rs`): it opens in `default_cwd` in the closing tab's domain, and the old tab goes once it's there. The order matters: the mux drops an empty window at once, and a mux server ends a second after its last pane. With a seat, the Conductor's view then opens over the new tab, since the Conductor is what he kept; Esc or a click on the tab's row leaves it.

- It covers every close the GUI makes: the menu's Close tab, CloseCurrentTab (Ctrl+W), CloseCurrentPane on a tab's only pane (Ctrl+Shift+W), the tab bar's close button, and the Yes of their questions. Closing the same tab again while its replacement opens (Ctrl+W twice) opens no second one.
- And a last tab whose program ends by itself, `exit` or `lbterm cli kill-pane` (Lachlan's yes, 2026-10-25). Upstream's mux prunes a dead tab and then its emptied window at once; LBTerm's GUI installs a keeper (mux's `lbterm_keep`) that the pruning hands a window's last dead tab to instead, once, and that leaves it in place, panes and all, until its window's `lbterm_remove_tab` has opened the new tab and removes it. An Activity is held meanwhile, since the GUI quits on an empty mux while none is. In a mux server the server has already dropped its own window with that tab, so the spawn finds no such window there; `ClientDomain::spawn` then asks the server's window list, forgets the window it had, and spawns into a new one that stands for the same LBTerm window, as a new window's first tab does (the server, which ends a second after its last pane, is still up). A mux server sets no keeper, so it prunes as upstream.
- A window of several still closes with its last tab, LBTerm staying open in the others, and closing the window or quitting ends everything, as before. Without the pad (no `lbterm_conductor`, or the panel off), the last tab closes LBTerm, as in WezTerm.
- **Close marks** (since 2026-10-25, the Claude project's ask of 2026-10-24, Lachlan's pick). Closing a tab ends Claude Code mid-run, so its SessionEnd says `other`, as for a window closing or LBTerm going down, and the Claude project's Live conversations list kept every closed tab as "tab closed" (159 of them on 2026-10-24). So just before it closes a tab or pane Lachlan closed himself (the menu's Close tab and Close other tabs, Ctrl+W, the tab bar's close button, Ctrl+Shift+W, and the Yes of their questions, never a No), LBTerm writes `<session id>.closing` into `lbterm_close_marks` for each pane whose `claude_session` user var is a session id (`lbterm_extras::closing`; `TermWindow::lbterm_mark_tab` and `lbterm_mark_pane` in `last_tab.rs`). The Claude project's live-sessions hook forgets a session that ends with a fresh mark, like /exit. A window closing, quitting, the mux server going down and a last tab whose program ended by itself (the keeper) write none. Terminal's `lbterm.lua` sets the folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Megaproject\live-sessions`, with rawset); `New-TestConfig` clears it, so a test window never marks a real session. `lbterm\perf\close-marks.ps1 [-Mux]` checks it.
- `lbterm\perf\last-tab.ps1 [-Mux]` checks it end to end: Close tab without a question, F8 and F7 standing in for Ctrl+W and Ctrl+Shift+W, the new tab in `default_cwd` under the view, `exit` and `kill-pane` in the last tab, the window closing on WM_CLOSE, and a window without the pad closing with its last tab.

## What's kept where

- A tab's colour is kept by the GUI for its life, by tab id, so it survives dragging the tab to another window but not a restart (crash restore brings back folders and Claude sessions, not colours). Closed tabs' colours are forgotten the next time one is given.
- A tab's name is its mux title, as `tab:set_title()` sets it from Lua.

## Where it is

- `lbterm-extras/src/tabs.rs`: the colours, when a press becomes a drag, the slot under the pointer, where a let-go tab goes (tested). `lbterm-extras/src/menu.rs`: the menu's rows from `Facts`, their keys, Close finished tabs' choice, Wait on…'s page, Copy link's words, the layout and keyboard steps (tested in `menu/tests.rs`; "The tab menu's model", above).
- `wezterm-gui/src/termwindow/tab_mouse.rs`: dragging, tearing off, moving between windows.
- `wezterm-gui/src/termwindow/render/tab_menu.rs`: the menu, its card in the menus' fill (#262634 at 55% over the window's background, `palette::menu_fill`) with one falloff of the panel's red light round it (`lbterm_menu_glow`, render/menu_fade.rs: the four stacked rings of 16, 9, 5 and 2.5% banded at 2x and went on 2026-11-05), drawn in z-layers 60 to 62, which the compositor gives a slot of its own in `menus` over the tab panel: it fades in on `pane_enter` and out on `pane_exit`, and its highlight glides between rows on `hover` (render/menu_fade.rs, `Slot::Tab`; NEW-TAB.md, section 6). `wezterm-gui/src/tabcolors.rs`: the colours given. A flaw seen 2026-11-05, in every build: the "no colour" swatch's hollow ring is a ring and a smaller disc in the card's colour, each four corner sprites a few pixels across (`lbterm_menu_rounded`, its radius floored), and at that size they don't meet, so a cross of the ring's grey shows through the disc; with the compositor's card surface (feat/new-tab-menus) the gaps fall on other pixels (20 pixels of that swatch differ from the build without it, nothing else in the card's first rows). A disc drawn as one shape would mend it.
- `wezterm-gui/src/termwindow/last_tab.rs`: the last tab giving way to a new one while the pad stays, with `mux/src/lbterm_keep.rs` (a last tab whose program ended, kept for it) and the spawn's new-window fallback in `wezterm-client/src/domain.rs`; `TermWindow::tab_panel_fold` (`render/tab_panel.rs`): the panel folding before a question.
- The hooks: `mouseevent.rs` (the tab bar), `render/tab_panel.rs` (the panel, the pad's ring), `keyevent.rs` (the menu's keys before any overlay's), `paint.rs` (the menu after modals).
- `lbterm\perf\tab-mouse.ps1` (`-Mux` for a test mux server): every one of these in a test window, with no real input. It posts mouse and key messages to the window, starts only once the keyboard and mouse have been idle 3 s, and fails if the window is ever the foreground one. Test windows never take focus, and under `LBTERM_NO_ACTIVATE` a window's `focus()` does nothing, since upstream's types an Alt with SendInput to get the foreground.
